About Me

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Business Administration at National Chung Hsing University. My research focuses on recommendation systems, machine unlearning, and machine learning. I am passionate about building intelligent systems that help people discover relevant information and make better decisions.

I received my Ph.D. in Information Management from National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University in 2024. Prior to joining NCHU, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University and a Visiting Researcher at the University of Manchester.
