Research

My research sits at the intersection of machine learning and intelligent information systems. I am particularly interested in building systems that can understand user behavior, deliver personalized content, and respect user privacy. My work spans recommendation systems, generative models, and machine unlearning, with applications in e-commerce, social media, news, and short video platforms.

A central theme across my work is combining heterogeneous data sources — including text, images, social signals, and user interaction history — to build more accurate and robust models. I also explore how these models can be made privacy-aware through selective forgetting mechanisms.

Research Areas

  • Recommendation Systems — I develop personalized recommendation models using multimodal, graph-based, and generative approaches. My work covers a wide range of domains including games, music, news, hashtags, and short videos, with a focus on leveraging social and content signals together.
  • Generative Models for Information Systems — I apply GANs, VAEs, and contrastive learning to tasks such as tag recommendation, crowdfunding prediction, talent matching, and data augmentation. I am particularly interested in how generative models can address data sparsity and cold-start problems.
  • Machine Unlearning — My recent research focuses on recommendation unlearning, enabling trained models to selectively forget specific user data without full retraining. This has direct applications in privacy protection, the right to be forgotten, and data governance in AI systems.
  • Predictive Modeling and Data Analytics — I have applied machine learning to forecasting problems including air pollution prediction, electricity load forecasting, motor fault detection, and agricultural price prediction, using models such as LSTM, RNN-based autoencoders, and ensemble methods.
