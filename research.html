Research

My research lies at the intersection of machine learning and information systems. I develop intelligent algorithms and frameworks to address real-world challenges in recommendation, prediction, and data-driven decision making.

Research Areas

  • Recommendation Systems — multimodal, graph-based, and generative approaches to personalized recommendation
  • Deep Learning — neural architectures including GANs, VAEs, attention networks, and contrastive learning
  • Machine Learning — ensemble methods, resampling strategies, and predictive modeling
  • Data Analytics — time-series forecasting, NLP, and applied AI in industry settings
